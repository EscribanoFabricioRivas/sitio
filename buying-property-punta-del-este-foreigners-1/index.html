Buying Property in Punta del Este: Local Notarial Guidance Before You Commit

Uruguay offers foreign buyers a stable legal framework and the same property rights available to local owners. In Punta del Este, I provide local notarial guidance to review documentation, clarify the terms between the parties and structure the purchase before you commit funds.

Punta del Este · Uruguay

A property purchase begins with a clear legal foundation

Punta del Este attracts buyers planning a second home, an investment property or a lasting connection with Uruguay. Uruguay provides a reliable framework for foreign ownership, and international buyers can acquire real estate under the same general ownership framework available to Uruguayan buyers.

That framework becomes practical through local legal work. A property purchase requires a clear reading of the property documentation, the legal position of the parties, the conditions of the negotiation and the commitments that will be assumed from the reservation onward.

  • Property documentation and relevant legal background.
  • Title and the position of the parties involved.
  • Price, payment arrangements, timing and conditions.
  • The legal path from reservation to the next stage of the transaction.

My role as notary

Local notarial guidance gives the transaction structure

My role begins before the first binding document is signed. I study the available property documentation, review title and relevant legal background, identify the points that require definition and help organise the legal path of the transaction.

This work gives you a clear basis for deciding how to proceed with a reservation, a promise to purchase or the final deed. The purpose is to bring the property, the documentation and the agreed terms into a consistent legal structure before commitments are made.

  • Personal review of the available documentation.
  • Clear legal structure for the agreed terms.
  • Coordination of the documentary steps of the purchase.
  • Direct notarial support throughout the transaction.

Clear commitments

Clear conversations lead to a well-structured purchase

A sound property transaction depends on clear communication between buyer, seller, real estate agents and the professionals involved. I help bring those conversations into a legal and documentary structure that reflects what the parties intend to do.

The property, agreed price, payment arrangements, timing and conditions must be understood in the same transaction. This coordination gives the parties a clear framework for the reservation, the promise to purchase and the documentation that follows.

When formal representation is appropriate, a power of attorney may become part of the legal structure of the operation. Its scope must correspond to the property, the parties and the specific acts that require representation.
