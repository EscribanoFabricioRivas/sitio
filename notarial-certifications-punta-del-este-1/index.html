Criteria · Notarial certifications

Notarial certifications in Punta del Este and Maldonado for documents used in Uruguay and abroad.

When a document must be presented to a bank, company, public authority or counterparty, its formal route matters. Notarial certification is coordinated according to the document, the signatory and its intended destination.

Documentary purpose

Certification follows the purpose of the document

A signature certification is requested within a specific documentary context. A bank, company, public authority or private counterparty may require a document to be signed before a notary and identified with the formalities applicable to that document.

The notarial work begins by defining the document's use, its recipient and the role of the person signing. That information determines the appropriate route and the formal steps that follow the certification.

  • Documents presented to financial institutions.
  • Private agreements and corporate documentation.
  • Authorisations and declarations before third parties.
  • Documents that will be used outside Uruguay.

Notarial intervention

Direct coordination before the signing appointment

The document, the identity of the signatory and the intended destination provide the basis for a notarial certification. Reviewing this information in advance makes it possible to coordinate the appointment and the documentary route with precision.

For clients operating from abroad, the relevant question is practical: where will the document be presented, who requires it and what step follows after signing? Direct contact with the notary keeps that coordination attached to the actual transaction, institution or instruction involved.

Each matter is handled according to the document and the purpose it must serve. The focus remains on formal clarity and on a documentary route that supports the next stage of the matter.

Cross-border documents

When the document continues outside Uruguay

A document intended for use abroad may continue through an apostille, legalisation, translation or another formal step. The sequence depends on the destination country, the receiving institution and the document itself.

Defining that route before signing gives the certification its proper place within the complete documentary process. For bilingual coordination and local execution in Uruguay, visit the Foreign Clients section.
